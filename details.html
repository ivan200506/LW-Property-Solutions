Where your $99 goes each month

Every item is clear and practical — you will see it and use it.

Two seasonal AC tune-ups a year

Once in spring, once in fall. Filters cleaned, refrigerant checked, outdoor unit inspected, drainage tested. Most air conditioners that fail in a heatwave do so because of neglected maintenance.

Whole-home leak hunt

Under every sink, water-heater joints, toilet bases, and washer hoses — each checked in turn. In New York's older buildings, leaks are the leading risk: repairing a pipe costs far less than a downstairs neighbor's damage claim.

Electrical safety check

Panel, outlets, smoke and CO alarms — all tested. Alarm batteries are replaced for you, so there is no need to climb a ladder.

Small fixes, $0 labor

A new washer, a tightened screw, a slow drain cleared — handled by the technician during the visit, with no labor charge. Parts are billed at cost.

Member priority scheduling

One call, and we are on-site within 24 hours on weekdays. Non-members are scheduled in order; a one-week wait in peak season is common.

20% off big projects

Water heater replacement and boiler work — members receive 20% off, and a single job can offset months of membership fees.

What's not included

We set out what's not included up front, so there are no misunderstandings later.

  • Parts and materials are billed at actual cost — verifiable against the original invoices. We never mark up materials.
  • Major projects (such as full repiping or boiler replacement) are quoted separately — your member discount still applies.
  • After-hours emergency visits (9 PM to 8 AM) carry a surcharge. The amount is always confirmed before we set out — never added after the fact.
  • Properties that are very old or have major structural issues may be beyond what we can take on. We assess every property in person, free of charge, and tell you honestly — if we can't take it on, we won't pretend otherwise.

Common questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Just give us 30 days' notice — no questions asked, no cancellation fee.

I already have a super / management company. Why do I need you?

Your building super looks after the common areas and the building as a whole. We look after everything inside your front door. When a pipe bursts in the middle of the night and the super can't be reached, we answer around the clock — that's the difference.

Do you cover Queens / Manhattan?

Yes. Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, and Staten Island are all within our service area. If your property is farther out, just give us a call to confirm.

Is Chinese-language service really okay?

From your first phone call to the technician at your door, everything is in Chinese. An English version of your service report is also available on request for your records.

Is the $99 per property?

Yes — $99 per property, per month. If you own three or more properties, call us to discuss bundle pricing.
