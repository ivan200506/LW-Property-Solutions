Annual service cap: the maximum value of services included in the subscription fee in one subscription year, calculated at our standard labor rates. Work beyond the cap is handled under Section 3.

2. What's included

Scheduled inspections (number of visits as in Section 1):

  • Heating and cooling maintenance check: filters cleaned, refrigerant checked, outdoor unit inspected, drainage tested.
  • Leak check: under every sink, water-heater joints, toilet bases, and washer hoses — each checked in turn.
  • Electrical safety check: panel, outlets, smoke and CO alarms — all tested; alarm batteries are replaced for you.

Labor-free small repairs (defined as follows):

  • Handled only during a scheduled visit;
  • Limited to small repairs that can be completed within 60 minutes per visit;
  • Maximum of 2 items per visit;
  • Labor is free; parts are charged at actual cost (see Section 3);
  • Does not include teardown or major overhaul work.
  • Priority scheduling: members are scheduled ahead of non-members.
  • Major-project discount: work beyond the small-repair scope is priced at the tier discount in Section 1, based on our written quote.
  • Water-heater maintenance (Standard and Premium, once a year): water-heater drain and inspection.
  • Emergency visit surcharge waived (Standard and Premium, once a year): one after-hours emergency visit per year with the surcharge waived.

3. What's not included

The following are not included in the subscription fee. Where we can help, we will explain the options and quote separately:

  • Parts and materials: all parts and materials are charged at actual cost, verifiable against the original invoices; we never mark up materials.
  • Refrigerant work: must be performed by EPA Section 608 certified personnel and is not provided directly under the plan's general services.
  • Gas work: not included in this plan.
  • New installations and equipment replacement (including water heater replacement): major projects (such as full repiping or boiler replacement) are performed by licensed contractors and quoted separately; your member discount still applies; labor-free small repairs do not apply.
  • Work requiring a government permit: not included in this plan; arrangements, if any, will be discussed separately.
  • Emergency visit surcharge (amount to be determined; Standard and Premium members get one waiver per year): for after-hours emergency visits (9 PM to 8 AM), if a surcharge applies, we will state it before dispatch, and will only dispatch after the customer confirms.
  • Work beyond the annual service cap: quoted at our standard rates or the applicable major-project discount, and carried out only after the customer confirms.
  • Age and structural limits: properties that are very old or have major structural issues may be beyond what we can take on. We assess every property in person, free of charge, and tell you honestly — if we can't take it on, we won't pretend otherwise.

4. How it works

  • Free first visit: the first visit is free of charge and takes about 20 minutes; after the visit you receive a written Home Health Report. Even if you do not join the plan, the report is yours to keep, with no obligation.
  • Step 1 — Book: the customer books a visit by phone or email.
  • Step 2 — Visit and inspection: we attend at the booked time and inspect as set out in the plan.
  • Step 3 — Home Health Report: after each inspection we provide a report with photos, findings, and recommended next steps.
  • Step 4 — Written quote and confirmation: follow-up work is quoted in writing. We schedule it only after the customer confirms. No chargeable work is carried out without confirmation.

5. Fees and payment

  • Payment method: online payment via Stripe, monthly or annual.
  • Auto-renewal: the subscription renews automatically unless the customer cancels before the renewal date.
  • Monthly cancellation: may be cancelled at any time with 30 days' notice, effective at the end of the paid period; amounts already paid are not refunded.
  • Annual cancellation refunds: full refund within 7 days; after 7 days, refund pro-rated by remaining months, minus the cost of services already used.
  • Late payment: if subscription fees are overdue, we may pause service until payment is received.

6. What we need from you

  • Provide access so our technician can enter all areas to be inspected.
  • Tell us about known issues, including past leaks, faults, and repairs already done.
  • Secure pets during the visit so work can be carried out safely.
  • Give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule.

7. Compliance and liability

  • We provide services only within the scope we are licensed and lawfully permitted to provide, and we make no claim to hold a license we do not hold.
  • Work outside our scope is performed by licensed partners.
  • Plan services are limited to the annual service cap in Section 1 (except Premium, which has no cap).
